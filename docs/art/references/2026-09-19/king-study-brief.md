Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a focused pixel-art KING REDESIGN study for the game Empire. The three supplied images are the user's actual authored art, and are STRICT identity and pixel-language references. Image 1 contains the old obese king needing redesign, a large sword man, the nearly finished small soldier, and the author's castle. Image 2 has the nearly finished cook with tongue sticking out and the stout character behind the counter. Image 3 is the nearly finished soldier, the PRIMARY anchor for outline, palette, simple block anatomy and comic toothy mouth. Create ONLY a redesign study for the KING in this precise homemade expressive comedic medieval style, not generic cute RPG art. Keep cream flat skin, sharply stepped near-black single-logical-pixel outline, small far-apart uneven eyes, intentionally foolish expression, large asymmetric wide toothy mouth and a recognizable broad pear-shaped fat body, thin short legs, little hands, blue cape, gold crooked crown. Refine him into a coherent compact silhouette, with a simple medieval short blue mantle over a cream tunic, plain belt, and very minimal gold trim. The reference soldier and cook must remain the style authority; do not polish away the awkward charming expression, turn the face round or anime, add a beard, realistic shading, rendering noise, or rich armor details. Composition: one neutral parchment background with only THREE king poses of the exact same design, clear spacing, common baseline, equal size: neutral foolish pride; nervous teeth-clenched fear; hurt lopsided grimace. No other characters, environment, text, labels, grid, ruler, UI or typography. Show as a crisp large nearest-neighbor pixel-art study, visibly coarse uniform pixel blocks, designed for eventual 64x64 logical-pixel authoring per pose. This is a design study, not an engine-ready spritesheet. Preserve the distinctive mouth-led facial acting and restrained few-color clusters of the user's originals.
